Vendor Security and Compliance Policy (fictional sample for demonstration)
All vendors processing company confidential data must complete an annual security review before contract renewal. Vendors must notify the security team of a suspected data breach within 24 hours of discovery. All confidential data must be encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to production systems requires multi-factor authentication. Vendor access rights must be reviewed every 90 days. Contracts must specify data deletion within 30 days after termination, unless retention is required by law. The procurement team must document approval before onboarding any new vendor. The compliance team reviews exceptions and records written approval.
