
Incident Response Policy

Employees must report suspected cybersecurity incidents to the security team immediately.

The security team must acknowledge critical incidents within 30 minutes.

High-severity incidents must be escalated to the incident response manager within one hour.

Confirmed data breaches must be documented in the incident management system.

An initial incident investigation report must be prepared within 48 hours.

All incident evidence must be preserved according to the company's evidence retention procedures.
